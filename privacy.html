Reid Laptop Backup

Privacy

This utility is operated by the account owner solely to preserve and restore their personal files. It uses Google's Drive API to upload, list, and retrieve the encrypted backup objects used by the app. It requests the drive.file scope, not unrestricted access to all Drive files.

Selected laptop files are encrypted locally by restic before upload. Google Drive stores encrypted backup objects and associated object metadata. The backup credentials and repository password are kept in operating-system-encrypted private storage outside public repositories. File paths, sizes, checksums, and restore receipts are maintained privately for recovery verification.

The utility does not sell data, use backup contents for advertising, or share them with third parties. Access is limited to the account owner's authorized backup and recovery processes. Originals are retained unless the owner separately authorizes deletion. Backup retention and Google authorization remain controlled by the owner.

This informational website receives no backup files, credentials, or OAuth tokens. It has no application forms or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process ordinary web-request information under GitHub's own privacy practices.

Contact the account owner about this personal utility. Updated October 7, 2026.
