Reid Laptop Backup

Terms

This is an account-owner-operated personal backup utility, not a public or commercial service. No public registration, paid plan, or service-level commitment is offered.

Only the owner-authorized files and Google account are used. A snapshot is accepted as verified only after the designated integrity and content-restore checks pass. A completed backup does not itself authorize deleting originals or erasing an operating system.

Google Drive and GitHub hosting remain subject to their providers' terms. Access can be revoked by the account owner. The owner retains control of backup scope, recovery credentials, retention, and migration decisions.
